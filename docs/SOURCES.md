# Public sources and photograph credits

The Uganda page uses original summary text informed by existing project materials. Private research files are not included in this repository. Public background reporting is linked below.

## Reporting

- [Stanford King Center, October 25, 2023](https://kingcenter.stanford.edu/news/undergraduate-research-assistants-help-expand-entrepreneurial-opportunities-refugees): entrepreneurship course, mentorship comparison, undergraduate fieldwork and the Makerere University Business School connection.
- [STVP, April 11, 2025](https://stvp.stanford.edu/news/refugee-entrepreneurship-action-stanford-impact-labs-fellowship-update): Chuck Eesley’s field visit, stakeholder interviews and program development.

These are historical reports. Their preliminary statements and plans should not be presented as current final findings or confirmed commitments. The two AI research directions on the website are in preparation.

## Photographs

All three files are real documentary photographs downloaded from the Stanford reports on October 29, 2026. They are not generated or stock images. The image bytes are unchanged; pages may resize them for display.
