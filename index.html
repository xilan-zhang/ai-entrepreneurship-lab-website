About the Lab

AI is changing the knowledge and support available to people who want to build a business. What does that change about the opportunities they see, the decisions they make and the ventures they create?

The proposed lab would study these questions through research on entrepreneurial judgment, human and AI support, and access to opportunity.

Entrepreneurial judgment

How people identify problems, question advice and consider alternative courses of action.

Human and AI support

How mentors and AI tools can help entrepreneurs develop and use their own judgment.

Access to opportunity

How access to expertise and local resources shapes who benefits from AI-enabled support.
