When AI agents take on much of a young company's work, how do founders divide it between people and agents? A multi-case study follows companies over time to see how and why they move between using AI as a tool and letting agents pick up the work.

Venture capital and strategic technologies

When a strategic industry faces cross-border technology restrictions, do investors pull back or move in? Using deal-level data on 19,485 startups, this working paper traces how financing, investor mix and patenting shifted after the shock.

Working paperUnited States

Industrial policy and climate finance

Does a long-term public commitment draw in private capital? Using financing histories for 7,271 climate-technology firms, this working paper studies how a major clean-energy statute changed venture investment and the way climate firms finance growth.

Explore the climate finance research

Figure: hidden Markov model of financing regimes, from the working paper.

Working paperChina

Battery subsidies and who enters

Between 2009 and 2025, 56 Chinese cities launched programs to grow battery manufacturing. This working paper asks how the terms of that support, and what firms must show before they can claim it, shape which founders enter the industry and what kinds of firms they build.
